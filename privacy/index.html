Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

The Max Means Business tool connects only to the channel owner's own TikTok account. It has no other users and collects no data from viewers or the public.

What is used

  • From TikTok: the access token and the account's open ID, given when the owner authorizes the tool (scopes user.info.basic and video.upload). They are used only to upload the channel's videos as drafts to that account.
  • Storage: the tokens are kept on the owner's private server, readable only by the owner. They are never sold, shared or used for anything else.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The authorization page below only shows the one-time code in your own browser; it does not send it anywhere.

Deleting data

Revoking the app's access in TikTok (Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions) ends the connection; the stored tokens are then deleted from the server.

Changes

This policy may change; the date above shows the latest version.
